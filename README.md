An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sorry, I Heard That Wrong"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-sorry-i-heard-that-wrong-1858c729): the funniest one-line apology a smart speaker gives after mishearing its owner, in 140 characters or fewer.

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> Sorry — I heard 'wake me at seven' as 'wake the neighbours.' They are up. You are not.
